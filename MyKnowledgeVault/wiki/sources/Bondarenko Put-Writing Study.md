# Bondarenko Put-Writing Study

"Historical Performance of Put-Writing Strategies" (2019), Prof. Oleg
Bondarenko, University of Illinois at Chicago — commissioned/hosted by Cboe
(cdn.cboe.com), 17 pp. Retrieved 2026-08-26; immutable payload
`.raw/captured/a2a02a35545890d6f9eaaecb0a967a0f951c8d8c895c6ed990ddb467743e92ee.pdf`. Commissioned by an interested party (Cboe
sells the products studied); treat as high-quality but not fully independent.

Key figures (June 1986 – 2018, ~32 years): the Cboe S&P 500 PutWrite Index
(PUT — sells ATM monthly SPX puts, cash-secured) returned **9.54%/yr vs
9.80%/yr for the S&P 500**, with standard deviation **9.95% vs 14.93%** —
a materially higher Sharpe ratio — and a max drawdown of **−32.7% vs −50.9%**.
Put-writing underperforms in sharply rising markets and earns its edge in
quiet and falling ones.

Evidence for [[Options Income and Orderflow Research]]: systematic index
put-writing, the closest studied analogue of the premium-selling doctrine in
the [[Tastylive Options Strategy Guide]].
