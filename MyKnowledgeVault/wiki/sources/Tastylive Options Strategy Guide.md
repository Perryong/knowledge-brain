# Tastylive Options Strategy Guide

"Options Strategy Guide" (TT1469, dated 2023-06-27), tastylive, Inc. — 39 pp.
by Nick Battista, Mike Butler, Katie McGarrigle. Educational/promotional
material with an explicit not-advice disclaimer (p.2). Captured 2026-08-25
from a user-supplied PDF; immutable payload
`.raw/captured/1cb904928f823266fbfa14507df3d7ddac9304925d4d3ffb97634bc9830134d2.pdf`.

One page per strategy (~30 strategies) with a fixed template: setup,
directional assumption, ideal IV environment, days to expiration, probability
of profit, max profit / loss / breakeven formulas, Greeks, profit target, and
defensive tactics. Core doctrine: sell option premium in high IV around 45
DTE, manage winners at 50% of max profit (25% for straddles/flies), defend by
rolling, and choose consciously between undefined-risk (naked put/call,
strangle, straddle) and defined-risk (verticals, iron condor/fly, lizards)
variants.
