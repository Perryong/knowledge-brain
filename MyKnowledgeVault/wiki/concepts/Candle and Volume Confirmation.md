# Candle and Volume Confirmation

From [[Investment Guide Notes]] (page 5). Supports rule 5 of the
[[Investment Entry Checklist]].

**Golden rule:** price tells you WHAT happened; volume tells you HOW IMPORTANT
it was. Never read price without volume. Candle body = price result (green =
buyers won the close; large body = big movement; small body = indecision).
Wicks = rejection (long lower wick = buyers rejected lower prices; no wick =
strong conviction). Volume = conviction (high volume = strong participation;
low volume = weak conviction / thin liquidity).

## The six combinations to memorize

| Combination | Reading |
|---|---|
| Green candle + rising volume | Strong bullish — buyers in control, breakout likely |
| Green candle + falling volume | Weak bullish — rally losing conviction, often near resistance |
| Red candle + rising volume | Strong bearish — distribution or panic selling, institutions exiting |
| Red candle + falling volume | Weak bearish — sellers losing strength, often near support |
| Long lower wick + low/medium volume | Support test — buyers defended; watch the next candle |
| Big candle + low volume | Unstable — liquidity-driven, not conviction; often retraces |

**Near key levels.** At support you want long lower wicks, red candles with
shrinking volume, and green candles with higher volume than the reds. At
resistance: long upper wicks or green candles with shrinking volume = SELL
signal territory. Volume expansion rules: down candles + volume increasing =
distribution; down candles + volume decreasing = normal pullback; break up
**with** volume expansion = accumulation confirmed (bullish); break down with
volume expansion = bearish. Volume tells you who is in control.

## Accumulation vs distribution (high volume + flat price)

- **Near the bottom** → accumulation: candles overlap, many wicks, tight
  range, volume higher than usual. Retail is selling; institutions are buying
  everything without letting price rise. Often happens **before a breakout**.
- **Near the top** → distribution: price can't break higher, upper wicks,
  volume high but price stalls. Retail is buying (FOMO); institutions are
  selling into strength, capping price. Often happens **before a drop**.

You cannot confirm which is building from one candle — confirmation comes from
what happens next: break up with volume expansion = accumulation (bullish);
break down with volume expansion = distribution (bearish). Volume builds
first; price moves later.

## Institution involvement: Volume Ratio + Turnover + Candle

- **Volume Ratio (VR)** — how active today is vs normal days.
- **Turnover** — how much of the company's shares changed hands today.
- **Candle color** — who won the close (buyers vs sellers).

| Volume Ratio | Turnover | Candle | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| < 1.0 | < 0.5% | any | Quiet, low interest — ignore | Wait |
| ~1.0 | < 1.0% | mixed | Retail only; normal accumulation | Watch, don't rush |
| 1.0–1.5 | 0.5–1.0% | green | Institutions active but calm | OK to scale in |
| 1.5–2.0 | > 1.0% | green | Institutions buying | Buy / add |
| > 2.0 | > 2.0% | green | Strong mark-up / breakout | Buy (momentum) |
| > 1.5 | > 1.0% | red | Distribution (selling) | Do NOT buy |
| > 1.0 | > 2.0% | green with lower wick | Institutions defending | Wait for confirmation |
| high | high | small candles | Absorption: accumulation or distribution | Prepare, don't chase |

**Strong BUY signals:** green candle, VR > 1.5, turnover > 1%, price near
support / VWAP / MA, institutions buying. **AVOID:** red candle + high VR +
high turnover — institutions are selling into liquidity.

**One-sentence master rule:** volume tells you if big money is involved;
turnover tells you if big money is *committed*; the candle tells you who won.
If price moves without volume, something is building — if all three align, act;
if not, wait.

**Top-down filter:** 1. Fundamentals — should I own this at all? 2. Trend —
is the sky bent up? (MA200) 3. Location — am I buying a pullback, not the
top? (MA10/MA30) 4. Timing — are buyers actually stepping in? (candle +
volume)
