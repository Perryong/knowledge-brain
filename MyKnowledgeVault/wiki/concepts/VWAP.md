# VWAP

From [[Investment Guide Notes]] (page 4). Supports rule 4 of the
[[Investment Entry Checklist]].

**Volume-Weighted Average Price** (single day): the "true average price" of the
stock for the day, weighted by how much volume traded at each price. It answers:
*at what price did most money actually trade today?*

Unlike an MA (which averages price only), VWAP averages price × volume, so it
shows **where the big money traded**, not just where price passed through.
Institutions can't buy all at once, so they buy below VWAP and sell above it —
VWAP acts as the **fair value line for the day**.

## The most important rule

- **Above VWAP** — buyers are winning today; most money is in profit;
  pullbacks tend to be bought.
- **Below VWAP** — sellers are winning today; most money is losing; rallies
  tend to be sold.

That's it — everything else is extra.

## Combining VWAP with MAs

**MA = trend (bigger picture). VWAP = execution (today's battlefield).**

Bullish setup (most common and safest): MA20 and MA30 sloping up, price above
MA30, VWAP sloping up. Entries: price pulls back to VWAP and holds, or dips
slightly below VWAP and reclaims it with a green candle — institutions
defending VWAP while the higher-timeframe trend is bullish.

Avoid buying when price is above VWAP but the MAs slope down, or when price is
far above VWAP (overextended).
