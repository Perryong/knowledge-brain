# AI Engineer: 889 Job Description Analysis

Data-driven definition of the AI Engineer role from **889 unique job descriptions** across major tech hubs. Published 2026-03-04. Confidence: **high** (large sample, all figures data-derived).

## Key findings
- **Definition**: "An AI engineer owns the design, evaluation, and production operation of systems built on foundation models."
- **95.6%** of positions are production-focused; only **4.4%** research-oriented.
- Role subcategories: AI-First (69.9%), AI-Support (28.5%), traditional ML/DL (<2%).
