# IMDA AI Verify

AI governance **testing framework and toolkit** launched by Singapore's Infocomm Media Development Authority (IMDA). Enables organizations to run **voluntary self-assessments** of AI systems via technical tests + process-based checks.

## Facts
- **Voluntary**, not a legal mandate (contrast with the [[AI Governance and Compliance 2026|EU AI Act]], which is binding). Confidence: high.
- IMDA states AI Verify **aligns with international standards** from the EU, US, and OECD. Confidence: high.
- Singapore published a **Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI**; an Institute was established in 2025 under IMDA for safety/alignment testing of high-impact and generative models, working with the AI Verify Foundation. Confidence: high.
- AI Verify can **generate evidence that supports but does not replace** EU conformity assessment. Confidence: medium.

## Why it matters
For an AI engineer working SG-market systems, AI Verify is the concrete governance harness you produce evidence for — test coverage, fairness/robustness checks, process documentation. Complements but does not satisfy EU obligations. See [[AI Governance and Compliance 2026]].
