# AI Singapore — AI Apprenticeship Programme (AIAP)

Government-funded applied-AI apprenticeship run by **AI Singapore**, placing participants inside real-world AI projects alongside experienced engineers/mentors.

## Facts
- **Duration**: full-time, cited as 9 months (some sources: 6 or 9). Confidence: medium (sources vary).
- **Stipend**: SGD **4,000/month**; fully government-funded. Confidence: medium.
- **Placement**: >90% of AIAP graduates placed in AI roles within 6 months (some sources say within 3 months). Confidence: medium (self-reported by AI Singapore — treat as program-published, not independent).
- **Graduate salary bands (2026)**: AI/ML Engineer SGD 5,500–9,000/mo; MLOps SGD 6,000–9,500/mo; AI Solutions Architect SGD 7,000–12,000/mo. Confidence: medium.

> [!gap] Placement and salary figures are program-published or aggregator-sourced, not independently audited. Treat as indicative, not guaranteed outcomes.

## Why it matters
AIAP is the highest-signal **SG-specific** proof-of-skill path for applied AI engineering — it converts 1–3 years of general ML experience into placement-grade portfolio work. Pairs with the [[AWS ML Certification]] as tiebreaker credential. See [[AI Engineer Credentialing Paths 2026]].
