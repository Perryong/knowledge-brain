# AWS Machine Learning Certification (MLA-C01)

AWS's machine-learning certification track, overhauled in **early 2026**.

## Facts
- The legacy **MLS-C01 (ML Specialty)** was **replaced by MLA-C01**, which covers modern agentic orchestration and generative workloads. Avoid investing study time in the retired exam. Confidence: medium.
- Approximate cost of the ML certification track: ~USD 300 (Specialty tier historically). Correlated with ~20% pay uplift for AWS practitioners in one 2026 roundup. Confidence: low (single aggregator, correlation not causation).
- **Positioning**: a **tiebreaker, not a hiring driver** — worth it if your career is anchored to AWS. Certs sit **alongside** a portfolio, never in place of it. Confidence: medium.

> [!gap] The "~20% pay bump" figure is a single-source correlation. Do not treat certification as a salary lever on its own.

## Why it matters
AWS demand is **high in both SG and global** markets (40.1% of the [[AI Engineer 889 Job Description Analysis|889 JDs]]). The cert de-risks the cloud-competence question for hiring but only matters layered on shipped work. See [[AI Engineer Credentialing Paths 2026]].
