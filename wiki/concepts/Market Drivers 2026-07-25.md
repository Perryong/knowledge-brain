# Market Drivers — 2026-07-25

> [!warning] Narrative = model synthesis, not fact
> The [[Market snapshot 2026-07-25|indicator table]] is computed and deterministic. Everything below is a synthesis of web sources, filed by the autoresearch step. Sources are cited; verify before relying on any claim.

Three themes explain this week's board.

## 1. The Magnificent-Seven split: NVDA vs MSFT
The dominant equity story is divergence *inside* big tech, which the snapshot shows as **NVDA +9.5% YTD (golden cross)** against **MSFT −19% YTD (death cross, below SMA200)**.

- Microsoft is now the **worst performer in the Magnificent Seven**, down ~24% on the year, dragged by fears over heavy AI capex and AI tools disrupting its own software franchise. It sits near its lowest level since 2023.
- Nvidia held up as the infrastructure play — investors reallocating toward "earlier-stage beneficiaries of the AI buildout." The valuation gap is stark: platform names (MSFT) trade ~25× trailing earnings vs infrastructure names (NVDA) ~57×.
- This is why the [[SPY|S&P 500]] can still be **+8% YTD** while its second-largest constituent falls: breadth and the chip complex carried the drag.

## 2. Gold and Bitcoin: the year's worst majors, for opposite reasons
The snapshot flags both **[[XAUUSD|gold]] (−6% YTD)** and **[[BTCUSD|BTC]] (−28% YTD)** below their 200-day averages — corroborated by Bilello's July read (gold −7%, BTC −31%, the two worst major assets of 2026).

- **Gold** is down on the *year* yet holds a record **~$4,000+ absolute** — up >25% since early 2025 on central-bank buying, sticky inflation, a flat-supply "scarcity loop," and a Middle-East risk bid (reported Strait-of-Hormuz disruption). The YTD dip is a pullback from an extended run, not a breakdown.
- **Bitcoin** is the cleaner risk-off tell: −28% YTD with RSI ~51 (neutral), consistent with a repricing of rate expectations (see §3) rather than a crypto-specific shock.

## 3. Macro: the Fed pivot from cuts to hikes
The through-line under all of the above is a **hawkish repricing**:

- Markets have swung from pricing **~2 rate cuts** at the start of 2026 to **1–2 rate hikes** now — roughly a 1% swing in expectations. FOMC expected to hold at **3.50–3.75%** in July.
- **Core PCE 3.4%** — highest since October 2023, the 63rd consecutive month above the Fed's 2% target. Core CPI ~2.8% annual.
- Q2 S&P 500 earnings projected **+23–24% YoY**, with energy and tech leading — the earnings strength that keeps the index near highs even as rate-cut hopes fade. Some strategists warn speculation is at "extreme levels."
