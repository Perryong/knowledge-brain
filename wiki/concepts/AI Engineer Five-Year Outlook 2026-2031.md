# AI Engineer Five-Year Outlook 2026-2031

**Angle 4** — demand, pay, automation, and emerging specialisations. Salary/forecast figures are **US/global unless marked SG**, and confidence is **medium at best** (aggregator data; one source explicitly warns of an "AI hiring bubble"). Vendor/analyst forecasts (Gartner, WEF) treated as **low–medium** per program.md.

## Demand
- AI Engineer = **fastest-growing US job** (LinkedIn Jobs on the Rise 2026); tops LinkedIn's Skills on the Rise 2026. Confidence: medium.
- Most roles target **2–6 years** experience; entry-level rare (**2.5%** of postings target 0–2 yrs). Confidence: medium.
- WEF projects **net 78M** AI-related jobs by 2030; Gartner: **⅓ of enterprise software** includes agentic AI by 2028. Confidence: **low** (analyst forecasts).

## Pay (US/global — NOT SG)
- 2026 averages cited **~USD 206K** (+$50K YoY); national median ~$173K to $795K+ at frontier labs; base ~$140–185K, senior TC $300K+. Confidence: medium (ranges vary wildly by source). Do not treat any single figure as authoritative — `[unsourced]` at precision level.
- **SG** pay signal (from [[AI Singapore AIAP]] graduate outcomes): AI/ML Engineer **SGD 5,500–9,000/mo**. Confidence: medium.

## What's automating vs appreciating
- **Automating**: boilerplate coding, glue code, first-draft implementation (coding agents). The "write the obvious code" slice shrinks.
- **Appreciating**: system design, eval design, agent orchestration, security/governance judgement, and the ability to decide **what** to build — the non-automatable judgement layer (see [[AI Engineer Skill Checklist 2026]] domain 6).

## Emerging specialisations
- **Agent engineering** — ~90k agentic postings 2026, **+280% YoY**, bands **$185–320K**. Confidence: medium.
- **Eval engineering** — clusters at frontier labs (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepMind, Mistral, xAI); title may consolidate to **"AI Quality Engineer"** by ~2030. Confidence: medium.
- **AI platform/infra** and **applied AI product** — the two other durable branches.

> [!gap] Salary figures are the least reliable data in this research — sources disagree by 2–4×. Use for direction, not planning.
