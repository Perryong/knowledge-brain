# AI Engineer Role Scope 2026

**Angle 1** — how "AI Engineer" is defined in 2026 and where it diverges from adjacent roles.

## Definition
An AI engineer **ships products powered by foundation models** — owning design, evaluation, and production operation of LLM-based systems (Source: [[AI Engineer 889 Job Description Analysis]], 889 JDs, **95.6% production-focused**). Confidence: **high**.

Day-to-day: wire LLMs, RAG, prompts, and agents into applications; turn prototypes into observable, reliable services; integrate external model APIs under real-world constraints; own eval + monitoring. Confidence: high.

## Divergence from adjacent roles
| Role | Core question | Primary artifact |
|---|---|---|
| **Data Scientist** | "What does the data say?" | analyses, experiments, insights |
| **ML Engineer** | "How do we predict at scale?" | trained models + serving infra |
| **AI Engineer** | "How do we ship an LLM product?" | LLM apps: RAG, agents, prompts, vector DBs |
| **AI Platform Engineer** | "What do AI teams build on?" | inference infra, IaC, model gateways |
| **Software Engineer** | "How do we build the system?" | application code, services |

Confidence: **medium** (synthesized across role-comparison guides, not one primary dataset). The boundary AI-Engineer↔Software-Engineer is blurriest in SG (see [[AI Engineer Demanded Skillset 2026]] — SG expects full-stack).

## Seniority & placement
- Most roles target **2–6 years** experience; **4–6 years** most common (~12% of postings); entry-level rare (**2.5%** target 0–2 yrs) (Source: [[AI Engineer Five-Year Outlook 2026-2031]]). Confidence: medium.
- AI engineers sit **on product/platform teams**, not in research orgs (only 4.4% of roles are research). Confidence: high.

> [!gap] No single dataset cleanly separates AI Engineer from AI Platform Engineer; the split is emerging, not settled.
