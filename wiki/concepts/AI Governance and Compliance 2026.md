# AI Governance and Compliance 2026 (SG + EU)

**Angle 3 (compliance layer)** — the regulatory context an AI engineer builds within.

## Singapore (voluntary, standards-aligned)
- **[[IMDA AI Verify]]** — voluntary self-assessment testing framework; aligns with EU/US/OECD standards. Confidence: high.
- **Model AI Governance Framework for Generative AI** — incident reporting "proportionate and calibrated"; cites the EU AI Act as a reference for risk categorization. Confidence: high.
- **MAS** — guidance on acceptable GenAI use in financial services; **Project MindForge** provides regulatory guardrails for experimentation (customer-facing, internal knowledge, code-gen). Confidence: medium.
- A safety/alignment **testing Institute** was established under IMDA in 2025, working with the AI Verify Foundation. Confidence: high.

## EU AI Act (binding, extraterritorial)
- Remaining provisions apply **2 Aug 2026**: GPAI model penalty powers, **Article 50 transparency** obligations, national market-surveillance authorities. Confidence: high.
- **High-risk systems (Annex III) deferred to 2 Dec 2027** — 16 months later than originally scheduled. Confidence: high.
- SG's AI Verify can **generate supporting evidence but does not replace** EU conformity assessment. Confidence: medium.

## Engineering implication
For SG-market work, governance is **voluntary but expected** (esp. banks under MAS). For any EU-touching product, the Act is **binding** and the 2026/2027 dates are hard deadlines. Build eval + documentation evidence continuously (see [[SSDLC for AI Systems]]) rather than retrofitting.

> [!gap] Regulatory timelines shifted during 2026 (EU "timeline relief" / omnibus). Re-verify dates before relying on them for a compliance plan.
